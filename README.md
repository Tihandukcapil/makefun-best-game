DAWNWISP — a wisp of dawn-light against the Long Night. Steer the wisp with WASD, arrow keys or mouse; your aura of light and your fading trail cleanse the shades that close in, cleansed shades drop embers, embers charge your Nova, and every cleared wave offers a choice of three blessings. Survive all 10 waves and the dawn breaks — then keep going in the Endless Night. Five kinds of shade, a Gloom Herald boss on waves 5 and 10, combos, synthesized sound, and a full loop from title to victory to restart. Pure static files, no build step, no network request.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
